Your Slippi account goes in this folder, as user.json.

Easiest (Windows): double-click "Copy my Slippi account to SD.bat" at the top
of this SD card. It copies the account Slippi Launcher on your PC is logged in to.

By hand: copy user.json from Slippi Launcher's files into this folder.
  Windows: %APPDATA%\Slippi Launcher\netplay\User\Slippi\user.json
  macOS / Linux: search Slippi Launcher's folder for user.json
  (see https://github.com/2gifts/SlippWii#your-slippi-account)

user.json is your Slippi login: keep it private, don't share it.
